================================================================================
ITEM DEFINITION TEMPLATE & EXAMPLES
================================================================================

An item is granted to a character for a battle and can be upgraded across three tiers.
Each tier adds an active combat effect that consumes charges or
an action to activate.

EXAMPLE 1: King Knight
================================================================================

ID: king_knight
VERSION: 2026-08-08
NAME: King Knight
DESCRIPTION: A true King fights alongside his subjects.

TIER_1:

  ACTIONS:
    ACTION_1:
      NAME: Exploding rats
      DESCRIPTION: Deal 5 Damage (5 Uses)
      CHARGES: 5
      MOD: DAMAGE
      EFFECTS:
