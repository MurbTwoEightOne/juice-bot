================================================================================
TEAM ACTION DEFINITION TEMPLATE & EXAMPLES
================================================================================

A team action is a combination move requiring specific class pairs/groups.
Multiple class combinations (Fighter+Support, Support+Utility, etc.) can have
different team actions available.

All participants spend their action for the round.
